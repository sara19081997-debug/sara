THE BEGINNING OF SUMMER

Back to my village 🌿

BACK IN THE CITY

Exploring Almaty together 🏙️

My friend from Semey came to Almaty. It was her first time staying in Almaty for such a long time.

So I decided to show her some of the most beautiful and interesting places in the city.

We started with the famous Medeu stairs, visited the Botanical Garden, explored the city at night, went to cinemas and my favorite places, and also went to Kapchagay.
